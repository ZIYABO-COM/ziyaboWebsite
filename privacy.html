Website privacy

Your information.
A clear explanation.

How this website handles browsing, prepared enquiries and career applications.

Updated 2 October 2026

Browsing

This website does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts or cookies in its supplied code. Fonts and photographs are served locally with the website. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and access logs, when serving pages.

Preparing an enquiry

The enquiry form prepares a message inside your browser. It does not submit your brief to a Ziyabo server, save it in browser storage or send a message automatically.

Choosing email passes the message to your configured email application. GymBinary enquiries are addressed to contact@gymbinary.com; other project enquiries are addressed to contact@ziyabo.com. You review and send the email yourself.

Choosing WhatsApp includes your prepared message in a link to WhatsApp. WhatsApp receives that link and processes information under its own policies. You choose whether to send the message there.

Enquiries and applications

Information you send is used to respond to your enquiry, discuss the requested work or consider your career application. Resume links open your email application; this website does not upload or store resumes. You attach and send the resume yourself.

External websites

GymBinary, WhatsApp and other linked websites or applications have their own privacy practices.

Questions and requests

For questions about information you have shared, contact contact@ziyabo.com. For GymBinary enquiries, contact contact@gymbinary.com.

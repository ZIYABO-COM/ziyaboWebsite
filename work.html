EasyLift ERP

From Excel-based processes to an integrated business system.

Live with business users
BusinessFleet & equipment rental
SolutionCustom enterprise ERP
DeliveryLive use + ongoing enhancements

The business context

Equipment rental involves commercial decisions, assets, people, service requirements and financial records. EasyLift needed to move beyond Excel-based processes into a system that connects these relationships.

The solution

Ziyabo developed a business-specific ERP bringing core workflows into one application. Business users are working in the live system while customisations and enhancements continue in parallel.

Ownership beyond delivery

Close collaboration, availability and ongoing support remain central to the engagement. The system evolves with the requirements of the people operating it.

Core solution coverage

  • Sales, CRM, quotations and approvals
  • Rental orders and operational planning
  • Fleet and equipment relationships
  • Workshop, service and parts usage
  • Inventory and warehouse operations
  • Users, roles, permissions and reporting

The broader ERP scope connects financial, procurement, vendor sub-rental and workforce requirements. Further modules and refinements follow the business roadmap.

Connected rental lifecycle
  1. Enquiry
  2. Quotation
  3. Rental order
  4. Operations
  5. Billing
